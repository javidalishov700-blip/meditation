Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 August 2026. Steady is a coping app for panic, anxiety, sleep, and related states. It does not diagnose or treat medical conditions.

No account, no server profile

Steady does not create a user account. Answers, PIN hash, session notes, and reminder flags stay on this device (local storage / app sandbox). There is no Steady backend that stores your mood, SOS taps, or onboarding answers.

What stays on the phone

  • Onboarding answers and reminder switches
  • Optional four-digit lock (hashed, not stored in plain text)
  • Passed-session notes if you use SOS
  • Language, theme, emergency-country override
  • A local support id for when you write to us — it is not uploaded

Network

Listening uses baked audio on the device. The app does not call OpenAI while you listen. A voice bake during development may use OpenAI; that key is never shipped in the app. Optional crash reporting runs only if a project owner configures it; it is off by default.

Purchases

Subscriptions, when enabled, go through Apple In-App Purchase. Apple processes payment. RevenueCat may see an anonymous App Store receipt to unlock Pro on this device. We do not receive your card number.

Emergency numbers

The crisis chip uses the phone’s country (timezone / region tag), not the UI language. You can override it in Settings. Steady is not an emergency service.

Children

Steady is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Contact

Questions: use the support id in Steady → Settings → Help. Copy that id into your note; it exists only on your phone.

Gizlilik

Son güncelleme: 16 Ağustos 2026. Steady panik, kaygı, uyku ve yakın durumlar için başa çıkma aracıdır. Teşhis veya tedavi sunmaz.

Hesap yok

Cevaplar, PIN özeti, oturum notları ve hatırlatmalar bu telefonda kalır. Steady’nin bunları tutan bir sunucusu yoktur.

Ağ

Dinlerken OpenAI çağrılmaz. Ses dosyaları cihazda durur. İsteğe bağlı hata raporu yalnızca proje sahibi açarsa çalışır; varsayılan kapalıdır.

Satın alma

Abonelik açıksa ödeme Apple üzerinden gider. Kart numaranız bize gelmez.
